🕵️ Crime & Heists
📖 DM Reference: Crime Rules
Allowed ChecksStealth, Thieves' Tools, Perception, Deception
DC 1050 gp payout
DC 15100 gp payout
DC 20200 gp payout
DC 251000 gp payout

Success Tiers:
• 0 successes = Fine equal to crime value + 1 week jail (or 25 gp fine)
• 1 success = Crime fails, but character escapes cleanly
• 2 successes = Partial success: half payout
• 3 successes = Full payout of the loot
*1 in 10 chance of complication per week.

🎲 Gambling
📖 DM Reference: Gambling
Allowed ChecksInsight, Deception, Intimidation, Gaming Set
DC Mechanic3 checks per week: DC = 2d10 + 5

Success Tiers:
• 0 successes = Lose bet + debt equal to bet
• 1 success = Lose half stake
• 2 successes = Gain half bet + stake back
• 3 successes = Double your money
*1 in 10 chance of complication per week. Minimum bet: 10 gp.

⚔️ Arena Fighting
📖 DM Reference: Arena Fighting
Allowed ChecksAthletics, Acrobatics, Weapon Skill, Con (+1 HD bonus)
DC Mechanic3 checks per week: DC = 2d10 + 5

Success Tiers:
• 0 successes = Lose fights, earn nothing
• 1 success = Win 50 gp
• 2 successes = Win 100 gp
• 3 successes = Win 200 gp
*1 in 10 chance of complication per week.
